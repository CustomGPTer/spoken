What Spoken does not do

Spoken does not stream: a free book is downloaded before it plays, though the first chapter can play while the rest arrive. It has no Chromecast, no syncing between devices and no books in languages other than English in its free list. It is not on Google Play, so you install it from this site and install new versions over the old one.

Where the others do more

Smart AudioBook Player works with Chromecast, keeps a list of characters for each book, and pauses by itself if you fall asleep. LibriVox Audio Books lists over 40,000 recordings in many languages, streams them and works with Google Cast. Both are on Google Play and update themselves.
