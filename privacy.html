Privacy policy

The short version: Spoken has no accounts and no servers, and nothing about you or your listening leaves your phone.

The short version

Spoken is a personal project by Jonathan Jackson, an individual developer in the United Kingdom. The app has no account, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking. The developer receives nothing from it and holds no data about you.

What stays on your phone

Your list of books, your place in each one, your bookmarks and notes, speeds, settings and listening stats are kept in the app's private storage on your phone. Covers Spoken finds or draws are kept there too. None of it is sent anywhere.

When Spoken goes online

Spoken works with no internet. It connects only in these cases:

  • Downloading a free book. When you tap Download, Spoken asks librivox.org for the book's chapter list and fetches the audio files and cover from archive.org. Those sites see a download request and your IP address, as any website would, under their own privacy policies.
  • The monthly look at the free list. Once a month, when you open it, Spoken reads a small file on this website to see whether the free list has grown, and fetches the new list if it has. Nothing about you or your listening is sent. On mobile data it waits, when downloads are set to Wi-Fi only. You can switch it off in Settings.
  • Links you tap. Check for updates and More apps open pages of this website in your browser.

Offline lock in Settings stops the first two completely.

Spoken does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or phone number.

Backups

A backup is one small file holding your places, bookmarks, speeds, settings and listening stats. It does not hold the audio. It goes only to the folder you choose. If that folder is synced to a cloud service, that service's own privacy terms apply to the copy.

Widgets, the lock screen and the car

The widgets, the notification, the lock screen and Android Auto show the title, cover and place of the book you are playing. Anyone who can see your phone or car screen can see them.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete it. You can remove a book, a bookmark or a download in the app. Uninstalling Spoken deletes everything the app holds, including downloaded free books. Your own audiobook files and any backup file you saved are yours to keep or delete.

Your rights

Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to request, correct or erase on a server. Everything is already in your hands.

Security

Spoken's data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Android's own app backup is switched off for Spoken, so your listening is not copied to a Google account without your knowledge.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies and no analytics. GitHub may keep standard web server logs, such as IP addresses, for security, under GitHub's privacy statement. App downloads and the free list come from GitHub's servers on the same terms.

Children

Spoken collects nothing from anyone. The free library is a public collection of older books, and some were written for adults.
