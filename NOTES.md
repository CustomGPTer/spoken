The first public version of Spoken, a free audiobook player that keeps to itself.

- Finds your audiobooks in the folder you choose, by a whole-phone scan, or both, and remembers your place in every book.
- Covers in a grid or a list, seven ways to sort, grouping by author, narrator or series, and switches to hide finished books and heard chapters.
- A player with the cover, skip buttons you set, a speed for each book, chapters read from inside M4B and M4A files, bookmarks with notes, skip silences and voice boost.
- Sleep timer for a set time or to the end of the chapter, with a fade, shake to add time, and a marker where it stopped.
- Subtitles, off by default, from an SRT, VTT or LRC file beside the audio.
- Plays with the screen off, with lock screen controls, three home-screen widgets, Android Auto and a car mode.
- A free library of 19,630 English LibriVox recordings on 25 shelves, listed inside the app and browsed with no internet. The list refreshes once a month.
- Listening stats, a backup file you keep, dark and light themes.
- No account, no adverts, no tracking. Offline lock stops Spoken going online at all.

Needs Android 8 or later. Install it over an older version to keep your books, places and bookmarks.
