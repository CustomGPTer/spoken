# Spoken

Spoken is a free audiobook player for Android. It plays the books on your phone with chapters, bookmarks, a sleep timer, widgets and Android Auto, and lists 19,630 free LibriVox recordings to browse with no internet. No adverts, no account, no tracking.

Website: https://customgpter.github.io/spoken/
