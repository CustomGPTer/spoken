Release notes

What changed in each version of Spoken. The newest is at the top.

Version 1.0 22 November 2026Latest

  • First release. Finds your audiobooks in the folder you choose, by a whole-phone scan, or both, with a clear explanation of which to use.
  • My books: covers in a grid or a list, seven ways to sort, grouping by author, narrator or series, and switches to hide finished books and heard chapters.
  • The player: the cover, your place in the chapter as a row of bars or a plain line, skip buttons from 5 to 90 seconds, a speed for each book, and a few seconds stepped back after a pause.
  • Chapters read from inside M4B and M4A files, and separate files played as chapters.
  • Sleep timer for a set time or to the end of the chapter, with a fade, shake to add time, and a marker where it stopped.
  • Bookmarks with notes, skip silences and voice boost.
  • Subtitles, off by default, from an SRT, VTT or LRC file beside the audio.
  • Plays with the screen off, with controls on the lock screen and in the notification, and three home-screen widgets.
  • Android Auto, car mode with very large buttons, and a choice of what double and triple presses on headphones do.
  • The free library: 19,630 English LibriVox recordings on 25 shelves and in 135 categories, listed inside the app and browsed with no internet. Download on Wi-Fi only if you like, and start listening as the first chapter arrives.
  • The free list refreshes once a month from this website. It can be switched off, and Offline lock stops Spoken going online at all.
  • Listening stats, a backup file saved by hand or once a week, your own cover from the gallery, dark and light themes, and colours from the cover.

Checking your download

The file for version 1.0 is called Spoken.apk. It is 7 MB (7,383,095 bytes). Its SHA-256 checksum is:

5fb233346d78917d974d02e26c26b2ade9159ba13781bfd5b83cfa352426c996

On Windows, run certutil -hashfile Spoken.apk SHA256 and compare. Every version is signed with the same key, so each one installs over the last and keeps what you have saved.

Older versions are on the GitHub releases page.
